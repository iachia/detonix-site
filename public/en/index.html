Frequently asked questions

How do I move my data to another device?
In Settings, under Data, tap “Export data” and send the file to the other device, for example with AirDrop or the Files app. There, open Detonix and tap “Import data”.
When does the Daily change?
At midnight UTC, the same for everyone. Your first attempt of the day is the official one.
What does No Guess mean?
Every field can be solved by logic alone, from the first cell to the last: you never have to guess.
I can't hear the sounds.
Sound effects are muted by the iPhone's silent mode. Also check Settings → Sound effects and the effects volume.
Does Detonix use the internet or collect data?
No. It has no accounts, ads or usage analytics, and everything stays on your device.
