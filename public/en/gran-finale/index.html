Detonix: Grand Finale is Tix’s second game: cut the fuses at the right moment so the fireworks go off in the order set by the program. A hundred story levels, Daily party, Quick play and Head to head, all offline.

For iPhone, iPad and Mac with Apple silicon.

It helps to know your device model, the iOS or macOS version and the Grand Finale version, which you find at the bottom of Settings.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my progress on another device?
If both devices are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, stars, records, aids and Daily party results come across by themselves. Settings stay on each device.
When does the Daily party change?
At midnight UTC, the same for everyone. Your first game of the day is the one that counts for the leaderboard.
What is the minimum number of cuts?
Every level says how many cuts are enough to set the fireworks off in the right order. The number is proved by a solver that tries every way: it can always be done with that many cuts, never with fewer.
Where can I play Head to head?
On iPad, on the Mac and on the open iPhone Duo, from Quick play: against a friend on the same screen or against Xit, at three speeds. On iPhone the field would not fit twice.
I can't hear the sounds.
Sounds are muted by the iPhone's silent mode. Also check Settings → Sound in the app.
Does Grand Finale use the internet or collect data?
No. It has no accounts, ads or usage analytics, and no servers. Your progress goes only through Apple's iCloud and, if you use it, Game Center.
