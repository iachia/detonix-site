Detonix: Grand Finale

Privacy

Updated on November 14, 2026.

Detonix: Grand Finale does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, ads, usage analytics or tracking, and no servers: your data stays on your devices, in your iCloud and, if you use it, in Game Center.

What stays on the device

Stars, best cuts and times of the levels, aids, Quick play records, Daily party results and settings are saved in the app’s own space on this device, and only Grand Finale reads them. iCloud or Mac backups copy them as for any app, if you turned them on. Deleting the app removes them from this device.

Progress in iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud on the device, Grand Finale keeps stars, records, aids and Daily party results the same on all your devices with the same Apple Account. Each device writes its share in the app’s own iCloud space in your account, under a random name that says nothing about you: it only goes through Apple, and we never see it. Settings stay on the device. If you reinstall the app with the same Apple Account, your progress comes back from iCloud.

Game Center

If Game Center is on (Settings, Game Center section) and you are signed in to Game Center on the device, Grand Finale sends Apple’s Game Center the achievements you reach and three scores: the stars you earned, the time of today’s first Daily party, if won, and the sum of your best times in the story. On the leaderboards other players see your Game Center name and these scores, according to your Game Center settings. Grand Finale receives nothing else from Game Center and doesn’t know who you are. Turn it off with the switch, or by signing out of Game Center in the device settings.

No network, no third parties

Grand Finale works fully offline: it has no servers, uses no other company’s services besides Apple’s iCloud and Game Center, and contains no advertising or analytics tools. It asks for no permissions: no camera, location, contacts or notifications. Only you see the code of a Quick play field: tell a friend if you like.

Apple

Downloads and updates go through the App Store, under Apple’s privacy policy. If in the device settings you chose to share analytics with developers, Apple may send us crash reports and aggregate statistics that don’t identify you.

Children

Grand Finale collects data from no one, so not from children either.

Changes

If a future version were to handle data differently, this policy will change first, and the app will say so before sending anything.
