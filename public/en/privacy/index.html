Detonix

Privacy

Updated on 12 November 2026.

Detonix does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, ads, usage analytics or tracking, and no servers: your data stays on your devices and, if you turn them on, in your iCloud and in Game Center.

What stays on your device

Games in progress, records, statistics, Daily results, your progress in Expedition, Xit and Crossing, and your settings are saved in the app's own storage on this device, and only Detonix reads them. iCloud or Mac backups copy them like any other app's data, if you turned backups on. You can delete them in Settings, under Data, or by deleting the app.

Sync with iCloud

If sync is on (Settings, under Data) and you are signed in to iCloud on the device, Detonix keeps your records, statistics, Daily results and your progress in Expedition, Xit, Crossing and Duel the same on all your devices with the same Apple Account. Each device writes its part to the space iCloud keeps for Detonix in your account, under a random name that says nothing about you: it goes only through Apple, and Detonix cannot see it. The current game and the settings stay on the device. Turning the switch off stops the device from sending and receiving; “Reset statistics” resets them on all your devices and in iCloud.

Game Center

If Game Center is on (Settings, under Data) and you are signed in to Game Center on the device, Detonix sends Apple's Game Center the achievements you reach and your best times: those of No Guess and that of the day's Daily, with 30 seconds added for each mistake. In the leaderboards other players see your Game Center name and these times, as your Game Center settings allow. Detonix receives nothing else from Game Center and does not know who you are. You turn it off with the switch, or by signing out of Game Center in the device settings.

What leaves, only when you ask

“Export data” makes a file with your saves and hands it to the share sheet: you decide where it goes. “Share” in the Daily sends a short text with the day, outcome, mistakes and time to the app you pick, without giving the field away. “Import data” reads only the file you choose.

No network, no third parties

Detonix works fully offline: it has no servers, uses no other company's services besides Apple's iCloud and Game Center, and contains no advertising or analytics tools. It asks for no permissions: no camera, location, contacts or notifications.

Apple

Downloads and updates go through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. If you chose in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports and aggregate statistics that do not identify you.

Children

Detonix collects no data from anyone, so none from children either.

Changes

If a future version ever handled data differently, for example with an online leaderboard, this policy would change first, and the app would tell you before sending anything.
